What We Do

End-to-End Agricultural Trade Solutions

From farm-gate sourcing in Canada to port delivery in Nigeria, we manage every link in the supply chain with precision and accountability.

Direct Sourcing

We source premium canola meal and bulk grains directly from certified Canadian producers, eliminating middlemen and guaranteeing product quality from origin.

International Freight Management

Full-cycle logistics from Surrey, BC to Lagos port — container booking, freight forwarding, customs documentation, and real-time shipment tracking under CIF terms.

Regulatory Compliance Consulting

Expert guidance on CFIA, NAQS, and NAFDAC requirements. Every shipment is backed by a Certificate of Analysis and Phytosanitary certification, keeping your imports fully compliant.
