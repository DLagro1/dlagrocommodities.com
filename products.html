Trade Terms — Incoterms 2020 (CIF)

All shipments are standardized on Cost, Insurance & Freight (CIF) basis under Incoterms 2020. This means DL Agro handles all freight, insurance, and export documentation to the port of destination — providing full buyer security and logistical efficiency. Documents include: Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Analysis (COA), Phytosanitary Certificate, and Packing List.
