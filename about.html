The Founder's Story

Built on Legacy.
Driven by Vision.

MBA CSCP Certified

The story of DL Agro Commodities Ltd. is built on a foundation established in the 1990s by Jenyo & Sons Agrochemical Ltd. Founded by Diran's father, that enterprise instilled in him the core values of integrity and the vital importance of agricultural trade. Today, Diran carries that vision forward — evolving a proud family legacy into a modern, global powerhouse.

A visionary supply chain leader, Diran brings over 5 years of hands-on experience in global logistics and procurement. Holding an MBA and a Certified Supply Chain Professional (CSCP) certification from the Association for Supply Chain Management (ASCM), he uniquely combines academic rigor with deep industry expertise to solve complex international trade challenges.

Having navigated high-stakes supply chains, Diran founded DL Agro Commodities Ltd. to serve as a reliable bridge between Canadian agricultural excellence and global market demand. His mission is to merge the "Old World" reliability of his heritage with 21st-century logistics — implementing world-class standards in transparency, biosecurity, and efficiency.

The DL Agro Model

How We Work

We operate on a Direct-Access Model — our clients and suppliers deal directly with decision-makers, ensuring speed, accountability, and clear communication at every stage.

Total Transparency

A clear paper trail from the Canadian farm gate to the Nigerian port. No hidden fees, no opaque logistics. Real-time documentation sharing at every stage.

Proactive Oversight

We don't wait for problems to arise. You receive real-time updates on market pricing, shipping schedules, and regulatory changes — before they affect you.

Long-Term Focus

We prioritize high-volume, recurring contracts over one-off sales. We grow by helping your business scale — building partnerships, not transactions.
